Click me The National Arts Theatre's unique exterior design was modeled after the Military Hat of Bulgaria.

In 1977, Surulere became the cultural capital of the Black world during FESTAC '77 (Second World Black and African Festival of Arts and Culture). Over 16,000 participants from 56 countries flooded into Lagos. The iconic National Arts Theatre at Iganmu was built specifically as the primary venue for this historic event.

Click me Surulere is one of the rare sports hubs in Africa with two massive stadium arenas—the National Stadium and Teslim Balogun Stadium—built directly opposite each other on the same expressway.

Surulere's National Stadium was the beating heart of Nigerian sports history. It hosted major historic matches, including the 1980 African Cup of Nations final where Nigeria won its very first AFCON title, defeating Algeria 3-0 in front of an electric crowd of 85,000 fans.

Ojuelegba is historically one of the busiest transport hubs linking Mainland Lagos to the Island. In 2014, Wizkid released "Ojuelegba," detailing his humble beginnings performing in local studios around Surulere. The song went global (with remixes from Drake and Skepta), turning a local Surulere bus stop into a globally recognized symbol of hustle and triumph.
